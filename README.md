## What It Does (`v1.1.59`)

- **Local & Cloud Model Hub (63 Curated Models + HuggingFace GGUF Hub)**: Run local models offline through [Ollama](https://ollama.com/download), pull quantized GGUF models directly from Hugging Face, or connect your own cloud keys (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Cerebras, Mistral, Together, Cohere, SiliconFlow, DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, NVIDIA). When no model answers, SMARAN tells you honestly instead of inventing a reply.
- **Speak & Voice Companions (`Amarya`, `Myra`, `Energy Core`)**: Hands-free voice sessions with interruption handling, photoreal moving worlds, Amarya's live facial expressions in English, Hindi & Hinglish, local **Kokoro** English TTS, online voices for Indian languages, **Gemini Live** calls with screen/camera vision, and offline wake words (`"Hey SMARAN"`, `"Hey Jarvis"`, plus `"Hey Amarya"` & `"Hey Myra"` on Android).
- **SMARAN Code & VS Code Extension (`v2.31.0`)**: Autonomous coding agent (`repo_map`, `multi_edit`, `run_python`, terminal, file checkpoints & one-click undo) plus a built-in **Browser** that opens local dev servers (`localhost:8000` / `3000` / `5173`), reads console & page errors, and tests UI changes while blocking the app's own backend port (`3003`).
- **Answers from the Web & Your Documents**: Multi-source Deep Research & Sparkpages with verified citations (`[n]`), and strict file-grounded RAG (`Answer from my files`) that says so when the answer is not in your files.
- **Pictures, Video & Music in the Free Cloud**:
  - **Images**: Zero-key **FLUX on Hugging Face**, zero-key **Pollinations** (with genuine PNG export and 1/2/4 batch modes), or a free **NVIDIA FLUX** key.
  - **Video**: Free **Wan 2.2** and **LTX-Video** on Hugging Face's shared GPUs, with 5/10/15s **Continue this video** extensions from the last frame, voice-over & soundtrack.
  - **Music**: Cloud **Lyria** / **MusicGen** on Hugging Face, plus a built-in local PC synthesizer.
- **Drive, Studio, Dots, Mail & Team**:
  - **Drive**: **Pages** (rich documents + **Record meeting** transcription & **Enhance notes** without a bot joining the call), **Slides** (`.pptx` export), **Sheets** (**Dashboard** summary & `.xlsx` export), **Two-Host Podcasts**, **Research Pages**, **Design Studio** HTML sites, and **Find & Download** agent.
  - **Dots**: Specialist autonomous helpers (**Researcher**, **Monitor**, **Writer**, **Developer**) with isolated Chrome browser profiles, **Take over / Hand back**, and schedule / webhook / incoming email / Telegram / Slack triggers.
  - **Mail, Team & Dispatch**: Gmail-style IMAP/SMTP **Mail** inbox, Wi-Fi **Team** workspace with member/viewer roles, and PC-to-phone **Dispatch**.
- **Privacy**: Zero telemetry, no ads, and no paid tier. Chats, memory, documents, and keys stay on your device unless you turn on a cloud feature.
