## What it does

- **Chat with the model you choose** - local models offline through
  [Ollama](https://ollama.com/download), or Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter,
  OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Mistral and more with your own key.
- **Speak** - a hands-free voice conversation that answers in the language
  you use: English, Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati,
  Bengali, Kannada, Malayalam and more.
- **Web answers with sources** - it searches, reads the best pages and cites
  every sentence.
- **Your documents** - answers from the files you add.
- **Work tools** - meeting notes, dictation into any app, pages, slides and
  spreadsheets, scheduled AI helpers (Dots), deep research, podcasts.
- **SMARAN Code** - an AI coding agent with approvals and one-click undo.
- **Pictures, video and music** in the free cloud.
- **Phone app** - on its own with your key, or paired with your PC over Wi-Fi.

## Privacy

Your account, chats, documents and keys stay on your own computer or phone;
there is no SMARAN server that holds them, and the app sends no usage data.
Something leaves your device only for a feature you turn on - a cloud model,
web search, an online voice - and then it goes to that service. You can report
an AI reply with its Report button, and delete your account in
Settings > Account ([how](https://smaran-ai.netlify.app/delete-account.html)).
Full policy: [smaran-ai.netlify.app/privacy.html](https://smaran-ai.netlify.app/privacy.html)
