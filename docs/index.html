01. Summary In short

SMARAN.AI runs on your computer or phone. There is no SMARAN server that receives your chats, files, voice or account. The app sends no usage data, analytics or crash reports about you. Something leaves your computer only when you use a feature that needs another service, and then it goes to that service, not to us.

This policy covers the SMARAN.AI desktop app (including the Microsoft Store edition), the Android app, the command line tool, the VS Code extension and this website. It is written by the developer, Shashwat Mishra.

02. What stays on your computer Local

  • Your account. The email address, optional name and password you create are stored only on this computer. The password and the answers to your security questions are stored as one-way hashes, not as text.
  • Google sign-in, if you use it, is between you and Google. Google tells the app your email address and name; they are stored on this computer. No SMARAN server is involved.
  • Your chats, memory, documents and files, and anything you give the app to read.
  • Design projects and Office media. Design prompts, drafts and canvases are autosaved in the local Design library. Supported images, GIFs and videos uploaded to Pages are stored locally for your account. Optional local image-reference uploads are kept on this PC and are not sent to a cloud image provider by that reference workflow. Exported copies are separate files.
  • API keys you save for cloud providers. Each key is sent only to the provider it belongs to.
  • Dots, scheduled jobs and settings.
  • Voice input. Microphone input is used for the transcription or assistant feature you start. Depending on the platform and feature, recognition can use a local engine or your device/operating-system speech service. A selected cloud voice service receives the audio or text needed for that feature under its own policy. Transcripts you keep in chats or documents remain part of your local workspace.

03. What leaves your computer, and only when you turn it on Your choice

  • Cloud AI models (for example Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere, NVIDIA, OpenRouter): your message and the context it needs go to the provider you chose, under that provider's privacy policy. With a local model nothing is sent.
  • Web search, when Web is on: your search goes to the search services the app uses, and the pages it reads are fetched from their websites.
  • Reading replies aloud: English is spoken on this computer. Other languages send the reply's text to Microsoft's online voices. The Gemini natural voice, if you choose it, sends the reply's text to Google.
  • Gemini Live calls, if you choose them: your voice, and your screen or camera if you share one, go to Google.
  • Pictures and video: optional local SDXL image generation on a configured compatible PC processes your prompt on that PC without a cloud generation request. Its model and runtime require separate downloads. For cloud generation (Google Gemini, Pollinations, Hugging Face, NVIDIA), your description goes to the provider you select. On a standalone phone, cloud requests are sent directly from the phone. Google native-audio video generates scene audio and dialogue together with the video. Optional narrator voice-overs send narration text to Microsoft's online voices. Generated media and prompt metadata are kept in separate image/video libraries on your device; you can rename or permanently delete the local file. Exported copies and provider retention are separate.
  • Photos in Design Studio are looked up on Openverse, or Pexels if you add a key, using words from your page's subject.
  • Telegram and connected apps (such as Slack or Notion): only what you send through them.
  • Optional Android phone actions: Android Accessibility is used only for requested phone help. After you confirm each screen-sharing request, one supported app window image can be sent to your selected Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq or Cohere provider to answer that question. It may contain private messages or photos visible in that window. Password/security screens are refused. No continuous screen capture or screenshot history is kept, and screenshots are not sent to the developer. Window capture requires Android 14 or later. Exact-label taps, scrolling and draft typing require confirmation for each action; messages and purchases are not automatically sent or completed.
  • Background voice on Android: when you enable it, a microphone foreground service listens for the wake phrase and displays a persistent notification with Stop. After activation, a bounded offline recognizer or the phone speech service transcribes the command; text questions go to your selected Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq or Cohere provider. Runtime provider credentials are held in the service only for that session and cleared when it stops.
  • Phone pairing: your phone talks to your computer directly over your own network.
  • Updates: the version downloaded from our website asks GitHub whether a newer release exists. The Microsoft Store edition is updated by the Microsoft Store.

04. What we do not collect Nothing about you

We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking in the app. We do not sell or rent personal information. The app does not send your chats, files, voice, keys or local account details to the developer. The one exception is content you explicitly submit as AI feedback (see section 05).

Website counters: the website sends a visit event or a download-click event, together with the file category, to our counter hosted at smaran-analytics.netlify.app. The event body contains no account identifier, chat, document or API key. Session storage prevents repeat visit counts in the same browser tab; no tracking cookie is used by this counter. Netlify and other services used to deliver the website receive normal connection information, such as an IP address and browser request headers.

05. AI-generated content Live generative AI

Replies, pages, pictures and videos are written by AI models in response to what you ask. They can be wrong. To flag a problem, open Settings → AI feedback, enter the content you want to flag, choose a reason and optionally add a note. Only the content, reason and note you submit go to the developer through the feedback endpoint hosted by Netlify; chat history is not automatically attached. Do not include personal information or API keys. Feedback is used to investigate the problem.

06. Keeping and deleting your data You are in control

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete a chat, or all history, in the app. To delete your account, open Settings → Account → Delete account and type DELETE (step by step). This removes account records, chats, documents and memory. Device-wide image/video libraries and exported copies are separate: use each library's delete control and delete any exports you no longer want. In earlier versions, delete Design projects and Pages with their own delete controls as well; account cleanup for these files is being corrected in an update. Uninstalling alone may leave local data on some platforms. To remove remaining local storage, use your platform's app-data removal controls or delete the app's data folder on your own computer. Data you sent to a cloud provider is kept according to that provider's policy.

07. Children 13+

SMARAN.AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The app can reach the open web and AI models that may produce content unsuitable for children.
