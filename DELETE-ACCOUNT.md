# Delete your SMARAN.AI account

How to delete a **SMARAN.AI** account (developer: **SHASHWAT MISHRA**) and
everything in it. The account was only ever kept on your own phone or
computer, so that is where it is deleted.

## In the app (Android, Windows, Linux)

1. Open SMARAN.AI, open the menu, then **Settings**.
2. Open **Account & Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete account…**, type **DELETE** and tap **Delete account**.

This deletes the account with its chats, documents, memory and settings. It
cannot be undone.

## What is deleted, and what is kept

- **On a phone used on its own:** the account, every chat and everything the
  assistant remembered, from the phone.
- **On a computer, or a phone paired with one:** the account and its chats,
  documents, collections, memory, plugins and activity log, from that
  computer.
- **Nothing is kept by the developer.** SMARAN.AI has no server that holds
  accounts, so there is no copy to delete and no waiting period.
- Messages you sent to a cloud AI provider (for example Google Gemini or
  OpenAI) are kept under that provider's own policy; delete them with that
  provider.
- A reply you chose to report (the Report button) stays in the developer's
  feedback inbox. To have a report removed, ask through the contact below.

## Deleting some data without deleting the account

Delete a single chat, or all chat history, in the app. Uninstalling the app
(or Android **Settings → Apps → SMARAN.AI → Storage → Clear data**) removes
the account and all its data from the phone.
