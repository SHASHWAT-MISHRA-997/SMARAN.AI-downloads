## What's New & Core Capabilities (`v1.1.81`)

- **2-Way WhatsApp Linked Device Gateway + Telegram & Slack**: Scan the official WhatsApp Web Linked Devices QR right inside SMARAN.AI (`Integrations & Gateways`), auto-reply to **Message Yourself** + `@smaran` / `/task` / `/dots` / `/status` (or all unread 1:1 DMs), and send direct messages — alongside Telegram and Slack Socket Mode bots.
- **Deep Analytics & Usage Telemetry**: Real-time SQLite audit/chat history, per-model inference throughput (`tokens/sec`) and latency tracking (`route_health.json`), hourly/daily activity charts, workspace asset counts, and server-side date filtering (`All time`, `Year`, `Month`, `Date`).
- **Local & Cloud Model Hub + HuggingFace GGUF Hub**: Run local models offline through [Ollama](https://ollama.com/download), pull quantized GGUF models directly from Hugging Face, or connect your own cloud keys (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Cerebras, Mistral, Together, Cohere, SiliconFlow, DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, NVIDIA).
- **Speak & Voice Companions (`Amarya`, `Myra`, `Energy Core`)**: Hands-free voice sessions with interruption handling, photoreal moving worlds, Amarya's live facial expressions in English, Hindi & Hinglish, local **Kokoro** English TTS, online voices for Indian languages, **Gemini Live** calls with screen/camera vision, and offline wake words (`"Hey SMARAN"`, `"Hey Jarvis"`, plus `"Hey Amarya"` & `"Hey Myra"` on Android).
- **SMARAN Code, Sandbox & Security, Cowork & Capabilities**: Autonomous coding agent (`repo_map`, `multi_edit`, `run_python`, terminal, file checkpoints & one-click undo), interactive Sandbox Mode (`STRICT` / `PERMISSIVE` / `OFF`) & timeout controls, Capabilities live self-test & emergency stop, Store MSIX `Run on startup`, and a built-in **Browser** for testing local web apps.
- **Answers from the Web & Your Documents**: Multi-source Deep Research & Sparkpages with verified citations (`[n]`), and strict file-grounded RAG (`Answer from my files`).
- **Pictures, Video & Music in the Free Cloud**:
  - **Images**: Zero-key **FLUX on Hugging Face**, or a free **NVIDIA FLUX** / **Gemini** key.
  - **Video**: Free **Wan 2.2** and **LTX-Video** on Hugging Face's shared GPUs, with 5/10/15s **Continue this video** extensions from the last frame, voice-over & soundtrack.
  - **Music**: Cloud **Lyria** / **MusicGen** on Hugging Face, plus a built-in local PC synthesizer.
- **Drive, Studio, Dots, Mail & Team**:
  - **Drive**: **Pages** (rich documents + **Record meeting** transcription & **Enhance notes**), **Slides** (`.pptx` export), **Sheets** (**Dashboard** summary & `.xlsx` export), **Two-Host Podcasts**, **Research Pages**, **Design Studio** HTML sites, and **Find & Download** agent.
  - **Dots**: Specialist autonomous helpers (**Researcher**, **Monitor**, **Writer**, **Developer**) with isolated browser profiles, **Take over / Hand back**, and schedule / webhook / incoming email / WhatsApp / Telegram / Slack triggers.
  - **Mail, Team & Dispatch**: Gmail-style IMAP/SMTP **Mail** inbox, Wi-Fi **Team** workspace with member/viewer roles, and PC-to-phone **Dispatch**.
- **Privacy**: Zero telemetry, no ads, and no paid tier. Chats, memory, documents, and keys stay on your device unless you turn on a cloud feature.
